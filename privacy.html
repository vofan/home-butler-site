Privacy policy

Home Butler is a private, self-hosted assistant used only by the household that runs it.

  • Access to Google Nest devices (Smart Device Management API) is used only to read the household's own thermostat state and change its settings on the owner's request.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: November 2026.
